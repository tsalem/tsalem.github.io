    Research Projects

    My research spans computer vision, machine learning and artificial intelligence, multimodal AI, and computing education. I am particularly interested in methods that connect visual information with temporal, geographic, acoustic, textual, and educational data, as well as AI systems that support learning and real-world applications.

    Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

    Food Computing & Vision-Language Models

    This research explores visual and multimodal AI for food understanding, including food recognition, segmentation, food-state recognition, and zero-shot recognition using vision-language models.

    Content-Aware Detection of Temporal Metadata Manipulation

    We developed content-aware methods for detecting whether image timestamps have been manipulated by comparing temporal metadata with visual evidence contained in the image.

    [PDF] [Code] [SUPP] [Project Page]

    Medical Image Analysis

    My medical imaging work applies deep learning to classification and segmentation problems, including breast cancer classification and chest X-ray analysis.

    [PDF]

    Geospatial, Temporal & Remote Sensing AI

    Learning a Dynamic Map of Visual Appearance

    This work models how the visual appearance of locations changes over time, learning dynamic representations from large collections of geotagged imagery.

    [PDF] [SUPP] [Project Page]

    Learning Geo-Temporal Image Features

    This project investigates representations that capture where and when an image was taken, connecting visual appearance with geographic and temporal context.

    [PDF]

    A Multimodal Approach to Mapping Soundscapes

    This work combines visual, geographic, and acoustic information to estimate and map environmental sound characteristics at geographic scale.

    [PDF] [Project Page]

    Visual Time Estimation

    Analyzing Human Appearance as a Cue for Dating Images

    This project studies how changes in human appearance provide visual evidence for estimating when photographs were captured.

    [PDF] [Project Page]

    Computing Education & AI for Learning

    Interactive Flowchart-to-Code Learning Environments

    My current work investigates how interactive visual programming environments can strengthen conceptual understanding in introductory programming. This research includes ChartCode, a flowchart-centric environment that connects program logic, visualization, and source code.

    NSF IUSE, 2026–2029: Improving Conceptual Understanding in Introductory Programming through an Interactive Flowchart-to-Code Environment. This collaborative project brings together researchers at Purdue University, Western Kentucky University, and Texas A&M University–San Antonio.
